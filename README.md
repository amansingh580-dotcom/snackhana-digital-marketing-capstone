# Snackhana: Digital Marketing Capstone

**An End-to-End Digital Marketing Strategy for a Modern Healthy Snacking Brand**

## 1. Project Overview

Snackhana is a healthy-snacking brand concept built around makhana-based products, including Salted Makhana, Roasted Makhana and Crispy Chips. The objective of this capstone was to develop an integrated digital marketing approach that could differentiate the brand in a competitive healthy-snacking market and guide potential customers from awareness to purchase and retention.

The project combined consumer research, competitive analysis, brand positioning, digital campaign planning, SEO, paid advertising, social media content and email marketing into a connected customer journey.

## 2. My Key Learnings and Takeaways

### Understanding Consumers Beyond Demographics

One of my biggest learnings was that effective marketing begins with understanding consumers' actual needs, routines and purchase motivations rather than targeting broad demographic groups.

I developed consumer personas representing different lifestyle needs, including a flight attendant looking for convenient, portable snacks and a healthcare professional seeking affordable, natural snack options. These personas helped connect consumer pain points with the product's proposed value proposition.

**Key learning:** Consumer personas become more useful when they influence positioning, messaging and channel decisions rather than remaining descriptive profiles.

### Turning Competitive Analysis into Brand Differentiation

The project examined both the broader healthy-snacking category and direct competitors in the makhana segment. The analysis highlighted an opportunity to move beyond ingredient-led communication and build a more distinctive lifestyle-oriented brand.

Snackhana's proposed positioning focused on everyday healthy snacking, convenience and modern branding rather than competing only on the ingredient itself.

**Key learning:** Competitive analysis is not simply about identifying what other brands offer. Its greater value lies in identifying what consumers may still need and where a brand can establish a distinctive position.

### Applying Segmentation, Targeting and Positioning

I learned how segmentation and consumer understanding can guide the choice of target audiences and shape a brand's positioning. Different consumer needs require different messages, even when the underlying product remains the same.

The project translated this understanding into a proposed brand identity and communication approach for Snackhana.

**Key learning:** A clear positioning strategy creates consistency across product communication, content, advertising and the overall customer experience.

### Building a Full-Funnel Digital Marketing Strategy

The project helped me understand how digital marketing activities connect across the customer journey rather than operating as isolated campaigns.

The proposed funnel covered four stages:

- **Awareness:** Introduce Snackhana and communicate the benefits of healthier snacking through educational content, social media and SEO.
- **Consideration:** Build interest and trust through product comparisons, ingredient information, nutrition-related content and testimonials.
- **Conversion:** Encourage purchases through product landing pages, promotional offers, reviews and clear calls to action.
- **Loyalty:** Support repeat engagement through email nurturing and relevant post-purchase communication.

**Key learning:** Each stage requires a different objective, message and performance measure. Reach and impressions are relevant to awareness, while conversions and repeat purchases are more appropriate for evaluating later stages.

### Learning SEO and Keyword Planning

I worked on keyword planning for healthy snacks, roasted makhana, fox nuts, low-calorie snacks and other related search terms. The project grouped keywords by intent and category, including generic, product-specific, lifestyle-oriented and purchase-oriented searches.

This helped me understand how search behaviour can inform content planning and paid search campaigns.

**Key learning:** Keyword selection should reflect both what consumers search for and where they are in the buying journey. High search relevance alone is not enough; the intent behind a query also matters.

### Understanding Google Ads and Paid Search Campaigns

The project included the planning of a Google Search campaign with a purchase-focused objective, conversion-oriented optimisation, keyword selection and ad copy development.

I also worked on connecting search intent with advertising messages and the proposed landing-page experience.

**Key learning:** Paid search requires alignment between the user's query, the ad's promise and the destination page. Campaign performance should ultimately be assessed through meaningful business outcomes rather than impressions or clicks alone.

The presentation included an estimated advertising budget and CPC assumptions. These were planning estimates rather than verified live-campaign financial results.

### Applying Meta Ads and Social Media Marketing

The project included Meta Ads campaign setup and the development of social media creative concepts for Snackhana. Instagram content was used to explore how product benefits, brand personality and everyday snacking occasions could be communicated visually.

This work helped me understand the relationship between campaign objectives, creative direction, audience relevance and platform-specific communication.

**Key learning:** Social media marketing is not only about publishing attractive content. Creative execution should serve a defined objective, whether that is awareness, engagement, traffic or conversion.

### Understanding CRM and Email Marketing

I developed a proposed email journey incorporating three key touchpoints:

1. **Welcome email:** Introduce the brand and encourage the first interaction.
2. **Browse abandonment email:** Re-engage visitors who explored products but did not proceed.
3. **Cart abandonment email:** Encourage users to complete an unfinished purchase.

The project also explored a lead magnet approach in which a healthy-snacking guide could encourage visitors to share their email addresses and enter a nurturing journey.

**Key learning:** Digital marketing does not end when a customer visits a website or clicks an advertisement. Email and CRM activities can help maintain engagement, address hesitation and encourage conversion and retention.

### Connecting Marketing Activities with Measurement

The project mapped different KPIs to different funnel stages, including reach, impressions, website traffic, engagement rate, click-through rate, conversions, purchases and revenue.

This reinforced the importance of choosing metrics according to the objective being evaluated.

**Key learning:** A campaign can generate attention without generating sales. Effective measurement requires distinguishing activity metrics from outcomes that indicate business value.

## 3. Project Execution and Deliverables

The capstone brought together several elements of digital marketing planning and execution:

- Consumer persona development
- Healthy-snacking market and competitor analysis
- Brand positioning and differentiation
- Full-funnel marketing strategy
- SEO and keyword planning
- Google Ads search campaign planning
- Ad copy and extension development
- Meta Ads campaign setup and creative development
- Instagram content and account analysis
- Lead magnet and email nurturing strategy
- Customer journey and workflow mapping

The project connected these elements into a proposed digital marketing system, from attracting relevant audiences to encouraging purchase and supporting ongoing customer engagement.

## 4. Key Outcomes

The principal outcomes of the project were strategic and practical:

- **A defined brand direction:** A proposed positioning for Snackhana as a modern, everyday healthy-snacking brand.
- **Consumer-led marketing decisions:** Personas connecting lifestyle needs and pain points with relevant product benefits.
- **A competitive differentiation approach:** An identified opportunity to move beyond commodity-style makhana positioning.
- **A structured customer journey:** A full-funnel framework linking awareness, consideration, conversion and loyalty.
- **Practical digital marketing exposure:** Experience planning search campaigns, keyword groups, ad creatives and social media activities.
- **A retention-oriented approach:** An email nurturing journey covering welcome, browse abandonment and cart abandonment.
- **A measurement framework:** KPIs mapped to campaign objectives and stages of the customer journey.

These outcomes reflect the project's strategy, planning and documented activities. They should not be interpreted as proof of incremental sales, advertising ROI or customer acquisition improvements, as those results were not established by the capstone presentation.

## 7. Reflection

This project strengthened my understanding of digital marketing as an integrated business function rather than a collection of independent tools. I learned how consumer understanding can inform positioning, how positioning influences campaign messaging and how each digital touchpoint can support a different stage of the customer journey.

The most valuable takeaway was learning to connect **consumer insight, brand strategy, digital execution and measurement** within one coherent marketing approach.
